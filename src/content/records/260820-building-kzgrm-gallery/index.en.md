---
title: "Building this site."
summary: We've started building Kazashimo's official homepage.
---

Kazashimo's works are scattered across many places — YouTube, X, and more.
It's great that people see them when they're first published, but as time passes they gradually drift away. There was nowhere someone who'd just discovered Kazashimo could go to see the older works all in one place.

So we decided to build a homepage where the works could live side by side.

The site is split into three parts: Works, Records, and News.

- **Works** holds things you can watch or play with — videos, photos, illustrations, games.
- **Records** holds behind-the-scenes stories, production notes, and event recaps.
- **News** holds what's coming up next — upcoming releases and appearances.

Keeping not just the finished works but also how they came to be might help people feel a little closer to them.

This homepage will keep growing alongside the works and records themselves.
